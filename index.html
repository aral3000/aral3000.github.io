Sriwijaya University (2026)

StyleGAN2-ADA vs. StyleGAN3 Cervical Image Synthesis & DenseNet121 Classification

Addressing class imbalance and annotated sample scarcity in Visual Inspection with Acetic Acid (VIA) medical images. Applied multi-stage StyleGAN2-ADA training on 666 VIA cervical images, achieving a best FID score of 59.49, and injected synthetic images into a DenseNet121 classifier to boost accuracy from 82.5% (Baseline) to 91.2% (Augmented).

FID Improvement: 89.23 (Stage 1) → 59.49 (Stage 6)
ADA Mechanism: Dynamic augmentation prob $p$ prevents discriminator collapse
CNN Classifier: Transfer learning with DenseNet121 on synthetic dataset
